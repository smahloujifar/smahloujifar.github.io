Saeed Mahloujifar

I am a research scientist at Google DeepMind. I am interested in security and privacy of machine learning and their interplay with foundations of cryptography.

Previously I was a research scientist at FAIR (Meta), and before that a postdoctoral researcher at Princeton University working with Prateek Mittal. I received my Ph.D. from the department of Computer Science at University of Virginia in the summer of 2020.
